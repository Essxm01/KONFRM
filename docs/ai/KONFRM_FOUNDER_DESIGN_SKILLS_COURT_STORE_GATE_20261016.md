## Platform & store compliance

6. During UI DESIGN assess current applicable Apple App Review Guidelines + Human Interface Guidelines and official Google Play Developer Policies + Android accessibility/Material conventions **per impacted flow**. Examples: platform back/gesture behavior, dynamic text, VoiceOver/TalkBack labels, touch targets (Apple recommended 44pt; Android 48dp), consent/privacy/user data, account deletion, truthful credentials/payments/permission states, platform-safe store metadata screenshots and misleading-function prohibitions.
7. Distinguish binding REVIEW/POLICY rule from ADVISORY HIG/Material design recommendation. Record exact source URL, guideline/section, observed screen behavior, PASS/OPEN/PRELIVE/BLOCKED and remediation owner. No generic "complies with Apple/Google" claim without tested native iOS/Android evidence. Android A56 screenshots are NOT an iOS acceptance test.
8. Account deletion and privacy disclosures must meet Apple 5.1.1(v) and Google Play account deletion/Data safety requirements **at release**; do not create a deceptive nonfunctional delete button. Accommodation/physical-service payment policies differ from in-app digital purchases (Apple 3.1.3(e), Google Play Payments); live billing/PSP integration and policy/legal confirmation remain PRELIVE, not assumed done now. Marketing/listing screenshots must reflect functions available in the version actually submitted, not unshipped mock transactions.
