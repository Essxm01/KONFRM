## 2. Mandatory practical design rule: BLACK/WHITE FIRST, BLUE EXCEPTION

- Default every NEW Customer visual concept to **white/light-neutral background, black primary CTAs and active selections, neutral-grey explanatory surfaces and text, canonical black logo/symbol, Cairo and strict Arabic RTL**. Real hospitality photography supplies natural non-UI color; photos do not convert UI controls into blue-themed panels.
- Blue is **NOT needed by default**. Allow a tiny carefully justified and governed interaction-semantic accent *only if* a specific current approved family/component requires it for affordance/focus/active navigation, clear contrast/accessibility, or truthful state communication. Never add blue because React/history used it, a default Flutter theme is blue, or an old token happens to be `#0059FF` or `#276EF1`.
- Blue is FORBIDDEN as visually dominant: no blue primary CTA, selected chips, calendar range blocks, pricing/payments summary card fills, accent cards, status banners, gradients, pill clouds, icon fields, large surface washes or decorative illustrations by default. No 'blue = trust' marketing shortcut.
- Do not blindly strip semantically necessary **error/destructive/favorite/accessible focus** signals. Those keep their actual governed semantic needs, role-appropriate contrast, and Founder-approved treatment. Any rare blue exception must be documented with purpose, screenshot and reference to the approved component; if ambiguous choose neutral/black draft and flag a Founder decision, not a new global token.
- **Priority:** newer explicit Founder visual direction / applicable mobile foundation / approved matching screen-family pixel evidence takes precedence over stale earlier blue instructions **for the conflicting color treatment only**. Other content of old UX specifications remains authoritative in its domain. Reconcile centrally in DESIGN_SYSTEM through an explicitly scoped, Founder-authorized follow-up; do not silently rewrite or delete historical source material, generated tokens or screenshots.
- **Role specificity:** This strictest near-zero-blue styling rule is VERIFIED for CURRENT Customer design. Owner/Admin should honor the broader mobile/desktop Design System and their own approved role palettes; do not apply an unreviewed blanket recolor to working Owner wallets, Admin audit/error states, or web tokens.
